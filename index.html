✦ A LETTER FROM YOUR BROTHER ✦
CONFIDENTIAL SIBLING DOCUMENT03 NOV ✧

Dear Chandana,

Happy Birthday! First of all, congratulations on successfully surviving another year with a brother like me. I know. Your patience deserves a national award.

I wanted to get you something normal, but then I remembered that normal is not exactly my department. So I went with a website. Because apparently sending a simple “Happy Birthday” message is too mainstream for me.

Jokes apart, I hope this new year brings you the kind of happiness that stays, the kind of memories you keep replaying, and plenty of little moments that make you smile for no reason.

Keep being your wonderfully weird self. Keep laughing, keep dreaming, and please continue tolerating my nonsense. It is a lifelong subscription, and unfortunately, there is no cancellation option.

Also, I have arranged a small reading corner for you below. Yes, I remembered the books you like. Don't get emotional; I still reserve the right to annoy you tomorrow.

With love, mischief, and unlimited sibling rights,
Your dearest brother ❤️
